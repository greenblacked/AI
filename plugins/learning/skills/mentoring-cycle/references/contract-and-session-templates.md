# Contract and session templates

Read at the contract session and when closing a session. Keep each document to one page; a longer contract will not be reread.

## The contract

```markdown
# Mentoring contract: <mentee> and <mentor>

Series: fortnightly, 45 minutes, <first date> to <date three months on>
Checkpoint: session held on <date>

## Goals (at most two)
1. By the checkpoint, <mentee> can <observable ability or decision>.
2. <optional second goal, same form>

## Ground rules
- Confidentiality: what is said stays between us. Exceptions: <safety, legal>.
- The mentee owns the agenda and sends it 24 hours ahead.
- The mentor asks before telling and does not evaluate for the employer.
- Either of us can end this at any time with one sentence and no reason.

## Ending sentence
"I think this has run its course for me." Neither of us will ask why.
```

Weak goals are topics ("get better at architecture"). Strong goals name an observable change ("lead the design review for the billing change and write the decision record").

## The agenda message

Sent 24 hours before the session.

```text
Since last time: <one line on what happened with the actions>
1. <question or decision>, and what I have tried
2. <question or decision>
Not for this session: <anything you want to park>
```

## The session shape (45 minutes)

| Minutes | Step |
| --- | --- |
| 0-2 | Review last actions: done, not done, learned |
| 2-37 | Agenda items, mentee speaking most of the time, ladder then advice |
| 37-40 | Mentor's one observation, offered as an observation |
| 40-45 | Mentee states and writes at most two actions with dates |

## The action list

```text
1. <verb phrase> by <date>
2. <verb phrase> by <date>
```

Send the list to the mentor the same day. Open the next session from it.

## The checkpoint

Ask three questions and write the answers: what changed against each goal, what the cadence was in practice (sessions held against sessions booked), and whether to continue, change focus or end. Record the decision and one reason.
