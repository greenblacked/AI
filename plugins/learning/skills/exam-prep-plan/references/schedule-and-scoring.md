## Diagnostic scoring sheet

| Domain | Blueprint weight | Questions | Missed | Miss rate | Weight times miss rate | Rank |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Miss rate is missed divided by questions in that domain. Questions in a practice exam rarely
match the blueprint weights exactly; use the blueprint weight, not the practice exam's mix, so
the allocation tracks the real exam. Where a domain has fewer than about eight practice
questions, treat its miss rate as rough and confirm with a topic quiz in week one.

## Worked allocation

Assume 60 study hours and four domains. Take the mock hours out first: two mocks of 2 hours
each and about 2.5 hours of review each is 9 hours, leaving 51 for study. Set a floor for any
domain with a weight of 30 per cent or more, here 10 hours, so a heavy domain with a low miss
rate is not starved. Each point of miss rate on a 40 per cent domain costs four times what it
costs on a 10 per cent domain, so a heavy domain with a low miss rate has most to lose if it is
starved.

| Domain | Weight | Miss rate | Product | Hours |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cluster operations | 30 per cent | 50 per cent | 15.0 | 25 |
| Networking | 20 per cent | 40 per cent | 8.0 | 12 |
| Storage | 10 per cent | 30 per cent | 3.0 | 4 |
| Troubleshooting | 40 per cent | 5 per cent | 2.0 | 10 |
| Mocks and review | | | | 9 |

Working it out: Troubleshooting takes its 10-hour floor, leaving 41 hours to share among the
other three in proportion to their products of 15, 8 and 3 (26 in all). That gives 23.7, 12.6 and
4.7 hours, rounded down to 23, 12 and 4. The 2 spare hours go to the top-ranked domain, which
makes Cluster operations 25. The total is 25 + 12 + 4 + 10 + 9 = 60. Then check that the mock
hours were taken before the shares and that no high-weight domain sits near zero.

## Skeletons by length

| Weeks to exam | Gap closing | Mixed practice | Rehearsal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | Days 1 to 12 | Days 13 to 21 | Days 22 to 28 (mocks fall at T-14 and T-5) |
| 6 | Days 1 to 22 | Days 23 to 34 | Days 35 to 42 (mocks fall at T-14 and T-5) |
| 8 | Days 1 to 30 | Days 31 to 46 | Days 47 to 56 (mocks fall at T-14 and T-5) |

For every length shown, T-14 falls inside mixed practice (day 14 of 28, day 28 of 42, day 42 of 56),
which is fine; the mock is also practice. T-5 falls in rehearsal.
If fewer than three weeks remain, say the plan is compressed and the go/no-go gate matters more.

## Error log and retests

| Date logged | Item | Domain | Cause | Fix | Retest 1 day | 3 days | 7 days | 14 days |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Cause values: did not know, misread, ran out of time. Compute the retest dates when you log
the item and put them on the calendar; a retest schedule held in your head is not run. A wrong
answer on a retest restarts that item's clock from one day.
