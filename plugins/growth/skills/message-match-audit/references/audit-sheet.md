A partial or mismatch on offer, price or next step outranks one on tone.

## Five-second test script

1. Recruit five people who have not seen the page and are not on the team.
2. Show the first screen on a phone or a laptop for five seconds.
3. Hide it and ask, one at a time: What is this? Who is it for? What would you do next?
4. Write the answers down in their words. Do not help or explain.
5. Count how many named the promised offer. Fewer than four of five means the match fails.

## Proof checklist

- Each number has a source or a date next to it.
- A named customer or logo appears with permission, near the claim it supports.
- A screenshot or short demo shows the product doing the thing claimed.
- Claims that cannot be supported are removed or softened.
