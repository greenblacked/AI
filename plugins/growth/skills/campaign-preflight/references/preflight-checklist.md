## First-wave watch list

- Bounce rate against the previous send of this segment.
- Complaint or spam-report signals, where the platform or feedback loops report them.
- Unsubscribe rate in the first 20 minutes.
- Replies that mention a broken link, wrong name or wrong offer.
- Link click errors in the platform report.

Stop and ask the owner if any is clearly worse than the previous send.
