## Questions that decide

1. Would a visitor with no knowledge of the site get what they came for from this page alone?
2. Is the data ours, licensed or scraped, and is the use allowed?
3. If two pages were shown side by side, would the difference be more than the name?
4. Does anyone search for this at all? Check the query volume of a sample of the page type before generating the rest.

## Outcomes

Ship: distinct data, answer in the first screen, enough pages above the threshold. Rescue: merge into fewer richer pages, or add data. Stop: nothing differs but the noun. Write the outcome and the reason in the worksheet so a later team does not regenerate the same thing.
