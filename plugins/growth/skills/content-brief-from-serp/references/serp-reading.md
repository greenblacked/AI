# Reading the results

## Contents

## Top-ten sheet

| Rank | URL | Format | Angle | Length | Non-text assets | Date | Covers | Misses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Fill one row per result and one row for each feature above the results. After ten rows, count formats. The majority format is the one searchers are being served.

## Mixed results

If no format reaches half, the query is ambiguous. Check variants of the query (add "how to", "best", "vs", "price") and see which version your product can answer. Pick that one and write down why. A page for the minority format may still rank, but expect it to be harder.

## Signs you cannot win

- Most results are tools or calculators and you cannot build one.
- Results are video or forum threads and you do not produce them.
- The top results are primary sources or government pages for a topic you can only summarise.
- Every leader has deep proof (original data, a product) that you lack.
