# Stage definitions

## Contents

- Stage template

- Monthly review agenda

## Stage template

| Stage | Entered when | Changed by | Evidence required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New | A form, call or chat creates the record | System | Source, campaign, click identifier, timestamp |
| Accepted | A rep confirms the lead is worth working | Sales rep | Fit confirmed and the rep's decision logged; first-contact time recorded as its own field, not a condition |
| Opportunity | A qualified need and next step exist | Sales rep | Need, route to budget, named contact, next step date |
| Closed-won | Contract signed or first payment | Sales or finance | Contract or invoice reference, value |
| Rejected | A rep decides not to pursue | Sales rep | Reason from the list, note if "other" |

Write the definitions in one page both teams sign off. Changes to them are dated.

## Rejection reasons

| Reason | Typical fix |
| --- | --- |
| Not a fit: size | Add company-size question or exclude the audience |
| Not a fit: region or language | Geo-target or add the region to the form |
| Not a fit: role | Change the offer so it speaks to the buyer |
| No need or budget | Qualify earlier in the ad or page |
| Existing customer or partner | Route to the account owner; exclude from paid audiences |
| Duplicate | Fix deduplication on email and domain |
| Student, job seeker, researcher | Move the offer away from the content they came for |
| Spam or junk | Add validation and bot protection |
| Unreachable | Check the phone and email fields and the SLA |
| Other | Free-text; review monthly |

## Monthly review agenda

1. Closed-won and pipeline by source for mature cohorts, 10 minutes.
2. Top three rejection reasons by source, 10 minutes.
3. Response-time compliance, 5 minutes.
4. Pick one change for the top reason and name its owner and date, 5 minutes.
5. Check last month's change against its reason, 5 minutes.
