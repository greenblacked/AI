# Standard template

Read this at steps 1 and 2, when inventorying existing values and writing the page.

## Contents

- Inventory table
- Standard page layout
- Closed list layout

## Inventory table

| Value seen | Sessions | Probably means | Fold into |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Newsletter / newsletter / email-newsletter | | the weekly email | source `newsletter`, medium `email` |

List every distinct source and medium pair from the last quarter, sorted by sessions. Record the date range of the export.

## Standard page layout

1. Purpose in one sentence and the owner of the standard.
2. The rules: case, separator, no spaces, no personal data in any value.
3. The five parameters with one example each.
4. Where internal links are covered (no tags) and what to use instead.
5. Link to the builder and the campaign register.
6. How to request a new value, and who decides.
7. Change log.

## Closed list layout

| Source | Medium | Channel it should report under | Owner of the source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| newsletter | email | Email | Lifecycle lead |
| linkedin | paid_social | Paid social | Paid team |

One row per allowed pair. A pair not here is not allowed. Review the list quarterly and retire pairs with no use in two quarters.
