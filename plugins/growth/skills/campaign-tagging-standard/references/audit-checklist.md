# Audit checklist

Read this at step 7, before the monthly audit.

## Contents

- Steps
- Deviation log

## Steps

1. Export source and medium (and campaign if the report allows) for the month.
2. Remove every pair on the closed list; what remains is the deviation set.
3. For each deviation, find a landing page and a date to trace the link: ask the owner of the likely source, check the campaign register, look at the referrer.
4. Classify: legitimate new value (add through the owner), mistyped link (fix at origin), foreign link (partner, scraper; add an edge rule if it recurs), internal tag (remove it).
5. Check the unassigned or other channel for any list pair that fails to map.
6. Record the count of deviation sessions and the share of total, so the trend is visible.
