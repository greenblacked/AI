# Working the search terms report

Read this at step 3, with the exported report open.

## Contents

- Export
- Sorting and thresholds
- Theme grouping
- Negative keyword rules

## Export

Export at least 30 days, ideally 90 for low-volume accounts, with columns: search term, matched keyword, match type, campaign, ad group, cost, clicks, impressions, conversions, conversion value. Note that the report omits queries too rare to list, so the total will not equal account spend; record the unlisted share.

## Sorting and thresholds

1. Sort by cost, descending, filter to zero conversions.
2. Set a click or cost threshold before judging a term: a spend of about one to two times your target cost per acquisition with no conversions is a reasonable point to act, and less than that is not yet evidence.
3. Separately list terms with conversions but a cost per conversion far above target. These need bid or match-type changes, not negatives.

## Theme grouping

Group terms into themes and total the cost per theme:

| Theme | Example intent | Usual action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Job seekers | careers, salary, internship | phrase negative at campaign or account level |
| Free or cheap | free, crack, open source | phrase negative, after checking the offer |
| Information only | what is, definition, how to | negative if the campaign targets purchase |
| Competitors | rival names | decide deliberately; separate campaign or negative |
| Unrelated product or service | shares a word with yours | exact or phrase negative |
| Wrong geography | other cities or countries | location setting, then negative |

## Negative keyword rules

- Add at the narrowest level that fixes the problem; use a shared list or the account level only for themes wrong for every campaign.
- Check each candidate against converting terms first.
- Match type matters: a broad negative matches all its words in any order, a phrase negative the words in order, an exact negative only the exact query. Negatives do not match close variants the way positives do, so list plurals and misspellings you care about.
- Keep a record sheet of what was added, where, and why, so a later drop in volume can be traced.
