## Definitions

| Measure | Definition | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time to first response | Median and 90th percentile from question posted to first reply by someone other than the author. A question never answered is still waiting at month end and ranks longer than every answered one; report the "ever answered" share beside the percentiles | Bot replies and staff auto-replies; exclude them. Percentiles over answered questions alone improve as more go unanswered |
| Answered in 24 hours | Questions with an accepted or clearly helpful reply within 24 hours / all questions | Replies that are only "same here"; use the same definition as ever answered |
| Ever answered | Questions with an accepted or clearly helpful reply / all questions | Decide how a reply counts and keep the rule |
| New member activation | Members who posted within 14 days of joining / members who joined | Joins from bulk imports or bots |
| Contributor concentration | Share of answers written by the top five answerers; number of distinct answerers | Staff counted separately from members |
| Returning members | Members active in month M who are active again at 30 and at 90 days | Define active (posted, not just read) |
