## Qualitative request

Send to sales and support each quarter, same wording each time:

1. Which deals this quarter involved a developer relations activity, and what was its role?
2. Which integrations or partnerships started from one?
3. Which support or onboarding problems were solved by a guide, sample or office hour we produced?
4. What did customers ask for that we did not provide?

Record the name of the person answering and the date, so claims can be checked.
