## Quick rules

- kcal = 4·P + 4·C + 9·F (alcohol: 7 kcal/g). Sanity-check totals with this.
- Restaurant version of any home dish: multiply home estimate by 1.2-1.4.
- "Creamy" or "crispy" in a dish name: bias to the top of the range.
- When a food isn't listed, decompose to nearest analogs by ingredient and cooking method.
