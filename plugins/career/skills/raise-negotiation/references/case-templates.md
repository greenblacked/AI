# Case and message templates

## Performance and scope case (dated evidence, last 12 months)

| Date | What I owned | Outcome | Next-level behaviour it shows | Who can confirm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [month year] | [project, decision or team] | [measured result] | [criterion from the level guide, or manager's words] | [name] |

Three to six rows is enough. Add a final row for work taken on beyond the role description, and one line on what the user stopped doing to make room. If there are no written level criteria, record the manager's own answer to "what separates this level from the next".

## Market case

| Source | Role and level | Location | Range or figure | Date | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [published band, survey, recruiter, peer offer] | [..] | [..] | [..] | [..] | [sample size, caveats] |

Three or more rows. State the median and the spread. Add the internal band for the user's level if known, and where the user sits in it (below, in, above). If the internal band is unknown, ask the manager or the compensation team; do not estimate it.

## Process questions for the 1:1

- When are budgets set and when are recommendations submitted?
- Who approves, and what do they need to see?
- Is there a band maximum for my level, and where am I in it?
- Is a promotion window open, and what is the criterion?
- What would make this request easy for you to carry?

## Written request

```text
Subject: Compensation and level review - proposed effective [date]

Thank you for the conversation on [date]. As discussed, I am asking for
[figure or title and level], effective [date]. The case is in two documents:
the scope I now own at [next level] (attached), and current market data for the
role (attached).

What do you need from me to get this approved, and when can we speak next
about it? I am happy to adjust the evidence to match what the approver asks for.
```

## Follow-up after a no

```text
Thank you for the answer. To make sure I understand: the constraint is
[budget / level / performance]. What would need to be true for this to become
a yes, and could we agree a date to revisit, say [date]? I would like the
conditions and the date in writing so I can work to them.
```

## Promotion without pay

Ask for the effective date of the new title and the pay change in the same message. If pay is deferred to the next cycle, ask in writing for the date and the figure range, and for the deferred pay change to be backdated to the title's effective date.
