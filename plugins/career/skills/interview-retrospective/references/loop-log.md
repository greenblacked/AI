Write the first four lines of each question before judging it.

## Recruiter question wording

Send once, within a few days, short and specific:

"Thank you for the time the team spent with me. If it is possible to share, was the main concern in the final round the depth of my system design answer or how I communicated it? Knowing which would help me improve."

Alternatives: "Which round gave the weakest signal?" and "Was the decision about level fit or about a specific skill?" If no reply comes, do not chase.

Compare any reply with your own classification. If they disagree, note both and weight the pattern across loops over either single view.

Summary after each loop: for each round type, passes over known outcomes, reported as passes/known alongside total attempts. Leave unknown outcomes out of both numerator and denominator; they still count for the cause column, since your own record is the evidence. Where a round type has fewer than about three known outcomes, use the dominant self-classified cause instead of the pass rate. Review at three loops.
