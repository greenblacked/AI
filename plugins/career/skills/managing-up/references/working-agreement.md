# Working agreement, update and bad-news templates

## One-page working agreement

```text
Working agreement: [name] and [manager]      Dated: [date]   Review: [date + 3 months]

1. Success in this role in six months
   [Two or three outcomes, each with a measure or an observable sign.]

2. What you most want to hear early
   [Examples: a slipping date, a customer complaint, a disagreement within the team.]

3. How you prefer bad news
   Channel: [message / call / 1:1]      Format: [the six-part shape]      Timing: [same day / next morning]

4. Decisions
   Mine, report afterwards: [list]
   Mine, consult first:     [list]
   Yours:                   [list]

5. What you want me to stop doing
   [Left blank for the manager to complete; the user's own guess goes in a comment.]

Cadence: weekly update on [day]; 1:1 on [day]; revisit this page each quarter.
```

Fill the user's draft answers in first. A manager who sees a blank page will answer in generalities; a manager who sees a draft will correct it.

## Worked example, decisions row

- Mine, report afterwards: choice of libraries within the team's approved list; ordering of work inside the agreed sprint goal.
- Mine, consult first: anything that changes a date promised outside the team; any new on-call commitment.
- Yours: headcount, vendor spend over the agreed limit, changes to the quarterly goals.

## Weekly three-line update

```text
Done:    [outcome, not activity]
Next:    [what lands next week, in order]
Blocked: [what, who or what unblocks it, by when] / nothing
```

Add a fourth line only when priorities changed: "Order is now A, B, C; D moved out."

## Bad-news message

```text
Subject: [Short factual title] - need a decision by [time]

What happened: [one or two sentences, facts only]
Impact so far: [customers, dates, money, people; state unknowns and when they will be known]
What I have done: [actions already taken]
Options: 1) [..., cost, risk]  2) [..., cost, risk]
My recommendation: [option and why]
What I need from you: [decision / air cover / a call to X, by when]
```

Send within a day of knowing. If the manager prefers a call, use the same six items as the order of speaking and send the text afterwards as a record.
