# Document checklist and clause questions

## Documents to gather for the lawyer

- The separation agreement and any cover letter or email, with the date received.
- The employment contract, offer letter and any later amendments.
- Equity plan rules, grant agreements and a current vesting statement.
- The last three payslips and the latest bonus or commission plan.
- The employee handbook sections on notice, redundancy, leave and benefits.
- Any restrictive-covenant or IP agreements signed separately.
- A note of the circumstances: dates, who said what, any complaint or protected activity before the exit.

## Owed anyway versus extra

| Item | Owed anyway? | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pay to the last day | Yes | Payslip arithmetic |
| Accrued, unused leave | Often, by law or contract | Contract and local rules |
| Notice (worked or in lieu) | Where the contract or statute sets it | Contract; whichever is longer |
| Statutory minimum severance | Where the jurisdiction sets one | Official source or lawyer |
| Vested equity | Yes | Plan rules and exercise window |
| Earned bonus already payable | Per the plan terms | Bonus plan |
| Expense reimbursement | Yes | Receipts |
| Additional lump sum, extra pay, continued benefits, acceleration, references | Extra, negotiable | This is the bargaining room |

## Clause questions

- Release: Which claims does it cover? What is carved out (accrued benefits, claims that cannot lawfully be waived, future claims)? Does it name individuals and affiliates?
- Non-compete and non-solicit: Duration, geography and scope? Is it new or already in the contract? Is the user paid during it? Can it be narrowed to named competitors?
- Confidentiality: Does it cover the agreement's own terms? Is there an allowance for telling family, advisers and tax authorities?
- Non-disparagement: Mutual? Does it carve out truthful statements to regulators and legally protected disclosures?
- Property and data: What must be returned and by when? Is personal data on devices addressed?
- Cooperation: Is there an obligation to assist in disputes, and is it paid?
- Payment: Amount, dates, instalments or lump sum, tax treatment, and any condition that lets the employer stop paying.
- Clawback: What breach triggers repayment?
- Governing law and costs: Which law applies? Who pays the lawyer's fee?
