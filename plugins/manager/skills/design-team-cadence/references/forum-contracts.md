or cancellation rule is not ready to schedule.

## Universal contract

```text
Name:
Purpose:
Primary output:
Owner:
Required roles:
Optional roles:
Frequency and timebox:
Pre-read location and deadline:
Allowed agenda outcomes: inform / discuss / decide / unblock
Decision authority:
Record location:
Escalation path:
Cancellation rule:
Health signal:
Kill/change criterion:
Expiry or review date:
```

## One-to-one

**Purpose:** maintain context, trust, feedback, support and growth between manager and
report.

**Primary output:** mutual commitments and surfaced issues, not project status.

Suggested structure:

1. Report's agenda.
2. Follow-up from prior commitments.
3. Feedback in both directions.
4. Context, growth, workload and support.
5. Decisions and named next actions.

Protect confidentiality while being clear about its limits: safety, harassment, legal
obligations, and material business risk may require escalation. Do not promise secrecy the
manager cannot keep.

Cancellation rule: reschedule rather than repeatedly cancel. A manager who cancels three
times has communicated priority more clearly than any engagement survey.

## Team planning or review

**Purpose:** accept a bounded commitment and its trade-offs, or inspect results and decide
what changes.

**Primary output:** selected scope, owner, success signal, dependency decision, and what was
explicitly excluded.

Required inputs: capacity reality, carry-over, operational load, dependencies, current
evidence, and kill criteria. Do not spend the meeting reading tickets aloud.

Cancellation rule: cancel when there is no meaningful scope decision or when evidence is
not ready; the owner publishes the consequence.

## Staff or leadership meeting

**Purpose:** make cross-team decisions, resolve ownership, allocate scarce leadership
attention, and escalate material risks.

**Primary output:** decision, accountable owner, date, and revisit trigger.

Agenda format:

| Item | Requested outcome | Owner | Pre-read | Timebox |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Remove team status from the spoken agenda. Discuss exceptions where another leader's
decision or help is required.

## Decision review

**Purpose:** improve a consequential decision before the accountable owner makes it.

**Primary output:** decision or explicit missing evidence, not consensus.

Require context, invariants, options including do nothing, consequences, reversibility,
recommendation, and confirmation mechanism. Use the `decision-record` skill for the
artefact itself.

State whether attendees advise, consent, approve, or decide. “Stakeholders” is not an
authority model.

## Operational review

**Purpose:** decide changes to service risk, reliability investment, support load and
operational ownership.

**Primary output:** accepted risk, corrective action, or escalation with owner and date.

Inputs: SLO/error budget, incidents, pages, recurring tickets, capacity, changes, known
risks, and overdue reliability work. Read trends; do not replay individual incidents.

## Retrospective

**Purpose:** change one part of the team's working system using evidence from the last
period.

**Primary output:** one to three experiments with owner, date and success measure.

Do not collect anonymous complaints without a path to action. Do not retain action items
across three retrospectives unchanged; cancel them or fund them.

## Skip-level

**Purpose:** sample organizational conditions and give people access to leadership without
creating a second manager.

**Primary output:** aggregated themes and leadership actions.

Ask about clarity, interfaces, workload, decision speed, learning and what leadership is
missing. Do not ask for performance judgments about the person's manager. Share themes
without attribution unless explicit permission or a safety obligation changes that.
