# The ask

Read this when writing the request: wording for each bucket, two shapes of ask, and the
end-of-quarter report.

## In this file

- Wording by bucket
- Fixed capacity share
- Bounded project
- End-of-quarter report

## Wording by bucket

- Pays back within two quarters: "We are doing these four items from our own capacity.
  Together they return about 40 engineer-hours per month from the second quarter. No
  decision needed from you."
- Blocks a named goal: "The checkout redesign due in March cannot ship on the current
  payment module without about six weeks of rework. Doing the module work first costs
  three weeks and removes the rework. This is part of the redesign's plan."
- Long payback, no blocked goal: "These six items cost about 25 engineer-hours a month
  between them and take over a year to repay one by one. They go in the 15 percent
  capacity share, ranked by payback, and we re-price them next quarter."
- Cosmetic: "Eleven items are real but have no measurable cost and block nothing. We are
  not asking for time for them."
- Long payback, large risk: "This item costs little each month but carries a 20 percent
  chance of a multi-day outage a year. The decision is yours: fund it, or accept the
  risk in writing."

## Fixed capacity share

State the share (often 15 to 20 percent of team capacity), the quarter, what product
gives up in engineer-weeks, the metric each funded item moves and the review date. A
fixed share is easier to approve than a list because it caps the cost. The team then
chooses items by payback within the share.

## Bounded project

State the start date, the end date, the engineers, the exit condition (the behaviour that
will have changed), the interest metric with baseline and target, and the point at which
the project stops if it overruns (for example half the estimate again). A stop rule is
what makes an executive willing to start.

## End-of-quarter report

For each funded item: baseline, result, hours recovered, what was learned. Include the
items that moved less than promised and the reason. Close with next quarter's ranked list,
updated from the new data. Send it to the same readers who approved the request.
