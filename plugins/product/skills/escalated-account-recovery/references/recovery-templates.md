# Recovery templates

## Contents

- Acknowledgement
- Complaint statement
- Recovery plan
- Weekly update
- Closing note
- Managed exit note
- Root-cause record

Each template is a starting shape, not a script. Replace every bracket; delete any line
that does not apply rather than filling it with something vague.

## Acknowledgement

Sent within 48 hours, from the owner, copying the executive sponsor. No explanation of
cause, no proposed fix.

```text
Subject: [Customer] — your message to [name], and who owns it

[Name],

Thank you for writing to [recipient]. We have read it in full, and I am taking
ownership of it on our side. [Executive sponsor] is copied and will stay involved.

Before we propose anything, I want to hear the whole picture from you and your
team, not only the events of [date]. Could we meet on [two options within five
working days], for 45 minutes, with [their decision-maker] if possible?

Until then, you can reach me directly on [phone / email]. Anything urgent that is
affecting your service now goes to me, and I will route it.

[Owner]
```

## Complaint statement

Written in the customer's words after the listening call, and sent back for
confirmation before anything is proposed.

```text
What we heard from [Customer] on [date]

Trigger
- [The event that caused the escalation, in their words, with dates.]

What had been building
- [Grievance 1, their wording, their example.]
- [Grievance 2 ...]

Impact on your business
- [What they said it cost them: hours, customers affected, internal credibility.]

What you have told us you will do
- [Not renew at [date] / reduce seats / escalate / claim credits], unless [their condition].

Is this right, and is anything missing? We will not propose a plan until you
have confirmed it.
```

## Recovery plan

| # | Commitment | Grievance it answers | Owner (side) | Due | Evidence of completion | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Fix / process change] | [Grievance] | [Name] (us) | [Date] | [What the customer will see] | Not started |
| 2 | [Provide logs, access, attendance] | [Grievance] | [Name] (customer) | [Date] | [Artefact] | Not started |

Below the table:

- **Executive sponsors:** [ours], [theirs]. They meet at kick-off, at close, and when a
  commitment is missed.
- **What we will not do:** [item, one-line reason].
- **Exit criteria:** [observable condition 1], [condition 2], [condition 3]. The
  recovery ends when both sides agree these are met.
- **Cadence:** weekly, [day, time], 30 minutes; written summary within one working day.

## Weekly update

```text
[Customer] recovery — week [n], [date]

Done:        [#, one line each]
On track:    [#, next milestone]
At risk:     [#, reason, what we are doing, decision needed from whom]
Missed:      [#, original date, new date, reason]

Exit criteria: [criterion — met / not yet, with the measurement]
Next meeting: [date]
```

## Closing note

```text
[Customer] — closing the recovery plan

Against the statement we agreed on [date]:
- [Grievance] — [what was delivered, date]
- ...

Exit criteria met: [each, with the measurement].
From here: [account owner] returns to the usual cadence; we will check in on
[date + 30 days] and [date + 90 days].
```

## Managed exit note

Used when both sides agree the account cannot be recovered.

```text
We understand you have decided to move away from [product] at [date]. To make
that as smooth as possible we will [data export format and date, transition
support, contract wind-down terms]. [Name] remains your contact until then.

We would value a short conversation about what we should have done differently.
```
