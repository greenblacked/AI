# Ladder, give-get table and request form

Contents: setting the bands, the ladder template, the give-get table, the request form, and the contract points to raise with legal.

## Setting the bands

Start from the audit. A workable first cut:

- The rep's own band covers roughly the discount most deals in the segment needed without any special justification, so ordinary deals need no approval.
- Each higher band covers a smaller share of deals, so approvers see the unusual ones rather than everything.
- The top band is rare enough that its approver can remember each deal.

Bands differ by segment where the audit shows segments genuinely differ. Do not copy another company's percentages; their list price, margin and sales motion are not yours.

## Ladder template

| Band (first-year discount, including off-invoice) | Approver | Deputy | Answer within | Give required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Up to the rep band | Rep | n/a | n/a | None |
| Next band | Named sales manager | Named | 1 working day | One give from the table |
| Next band | Named deal-desk lead or sales director | Named | 1 working day | Two gives, one of them term or prepayment |
| Above that | Named finance lead | Named | 2 working days | Two gives plus written competitive evidence |

Count the discount including off-invoice concessions, so a rep cannot stay inside their band by moving the discount into free months.

## Give-get table

Price each give in advance, as discount points it can earn, so approvers trade consistently and reps can quote it without asking.

| Give | Why it is worth something | Typical condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Longer committed term | Revenue certainty, lower renewal cost | Two or three years, no termination for convenience |
| Annual or multi-year prepayment | Cash now, lower collection risk | Invoiced at signature |
| Volume or seat commitment | Larger committed contract | Minimum committed for the term, not just the first year |
| Logo use | Marketing value | Written permission in the order form |
| Published case study | Sales asset | Delivered within a stated number of months, with a named customer contact |
| Reference calls | Sales asset | A stated number per year |

Gives to avoid trading for discount:

- Signing by the end of the quarter. It is valuable to the seller and teaches buyers to wait.
- A promise to expand later with no commitment in the contract.
- "Strategic logo" with no logo rights or case study written into the order form.

## Request form

A request missing any field goes back to the rep, not up the ladder.

```markdown
- Rep:
- Customer and segment:
- List price (annual) and requested net price (annual):
- Off-invoice concessions requested, with their list value:
- Total first-year discount and band:
- Gives offered, from the table:
- Evidence for the pressure: competitor and their written quote, buyer's stated budget, or procurement benchmark:
- Renewal price to be written into the order form:
- Date the customer needs an answer:
```

Keep every request, approved or not, in one place. The returned-for-missing-information count is one of the monthly figures.

## Contract points to raise with legal

This is procedure, not legal advice. Raise these with whoever owns your contract template:

- The order form states the renewal price, or the uplift from the first-year price, explicitly.
- The first-year discount is described as applying to the initial term only.
- Gives the customer committed to (logo use, case study, reference calls, prepayment) are written as obligations, not intentions.
- Any most-favoured-customer or price-cap clause a customer asks for goes to the top band, because it constrains every future deal.
