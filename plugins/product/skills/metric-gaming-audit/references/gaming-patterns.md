## How to use this catalogue

Each row is a metric, a cheap way to move it that harms customers, the counter-metric
that move would disturb, and a check that detects it directly. Use the rows as prompts.
The moves specific to your product, found by asking "what would I do if this number
were all I was paid for?", are usually the ones that matter most.

## Acquisition and activation

| Metric | Harmful shortcut | Counter-metric | Data check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sign-ups | Remove qualification, buy low-intent traffic, count unverified emails | Share of sign-ups reaching the activation event; week-4 retention by channel | Sign-ups by source with their activation rate; share with disposable email domains |
| Sign-up conversion rate | Hide pricing or terms until after the form | Refund and early-cancellation rate; complaints mentioning surprise charges | Read a monthly sample of first-week cancellations |
| Activation rate | Redefine activation as an easier step, or auto-complete the step for the user | Week-8 retention of activated users | Retention of activated users against the previous definition's cohort |
| Onboarding completion | Cut the setup that makes the product work | Support contacts in the first 14 days; time to first real use | Contacts per new account, by onboarding version |
| Trial-to-paid conversion | Auto-convert with a stored card and little warning | Refund requests and chargebacks in the first billing cycle | Chargeback and refund rate by conversion path |

## Engagement

| Metric | Harmful shortcut | Counter-metric | Data check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daily or weekly active users | More notifications and emails that pull people in without a task | Unsubscribe and notification-disable rate; sessions with no core action | Share of active users whose only activity followed a notification |
| Time in product | Slower flows, extra steps, infinite feeds | Task completion time; completion rate of key tasks | Median time for a fixed task, release over release |
| Feature adoption | Default everyone in, or move the old path behind it | Repeat use of the feature after week one; opt-out rate | Share of adopters who used it more than once |
| Sessions per user | Split one visit into many by shortening timeouts | Core actions per user | Session definition in the version history |

## Monetisation and retention

| Metric | Harmful shortcut | Counter-metric | Data check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Logo churn rate | Make cancellation hard: phone-only, hidden settings, repeated offers | Complaints and chargebacks citing cancellation; regulator or app-store complaints | Number of steps and channels needed to cancel, audited quarterly |
| Gross revenue retention | Lock customers into longer terms at renewal without value | Product usage of renewed accounts; next renewal's outcome | Usage trend of accounts renewed on term changes |
| Expansion revenue | Seat minimums or bundling that force upgrades | Contraction at the following renewal; discount depth | Seats paid against seats used |
| Average revenue per user | Raise prices on the least able to leave | Churn and downgrades in the affected cohort | Revenue change by cohort against usage |
| Revenue this quarter | Pull renewals forward with discounts | Next quarter's pipeline; realised price against list | Renewal dates moved within the quarter |

## Support and quality

| Metric | Harmful shortcut | Counter-metric | Data check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First response time | Automatic or templated acknowledgement with no help | Time to resolution; customer replies asking for an actual answer | Read a sample of first responses each month |
| Tickets closed | Close without solving; split one issue into several tickets | Reopen rate; contacts per customer for the same issue | Reopened and duplicate tickets by agent |
| Time to resolution | Close early and let the customer reopen as a new ticket | Repeat contacts within 7 days on the same topic | Customers with two tickets on one topic inside a week |
| Ticket volume | Hide the contact form, push customers to a forum | Help-centre searches with no click; public complaints; churn among customers who could not reach support | Contact-path changes in the release log |
| Bugs closed | Close as "cannot reproduce" or "won't fix" without contact | Bug reopen rate; duplicate reports | Monthly sample of closures read by someone outside the team |

## Satisfaction surveys

| Metric | Harmful shortcut | Counter-metric | Data check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NPS or CSAT | Survey only after successful moments; ask agents to request good scores | Response rate; scores from a random-sample survey | Survey trigger rules in version history; distribution of who is asked |
| Review rating | Prompt for a review only from users who rated highly in-app | Uninstalls; support contacts | The prompt's targeting logic |
