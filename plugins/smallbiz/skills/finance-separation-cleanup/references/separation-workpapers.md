## Tagging rules

| Tag | Use when | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business | A document shows it was for the business | Hosting invoice, client travel receipt |
| Personal | It is household or private | Groceries, rent, personal holiday |
| Mixed | One cost serves both | Phone plan, home internet, car, home office |
| Unsupported | No document and no way to tell | Round cash withdrawal, unclear transfer |

Rules:

- Tag the transaction as it happened. The account used does not decide the tag.
- A transfer between your own accounts is neither income nor expense; tag it transfer and
  match both sides.
- A client payment into a personal account is business income; record it and the account
  it landed in.
- Keep unsupported lines out of the expense and owner totals, but list unsupported
  inflows separately as possible income until a document turns up or the accountant
  advises.

## Mixed percentages

| Item | Monthly cost | Business % | Basis | Evidence | Period applied |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Bases that hold up: a mileage log of business against total trips for a sample period; a
call and data record; floor area of the workspace over total floor area, with the hours
it is used; a dated sample of working hours. State the sample period and re-measure
yearly or when the pattern changes. Use the method your tax authority names for home-office
and vehicle costs if it prescribes one; check its pages.

## Owner balance worksheet

| Date | Direction (draw / contribution) | Amount | Description | Document |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Totals:

- Draws (personal spending paid by the business): A
- Contributions (business spending paid personally): B
- Net owed: B minus A. Positive means the business owes you; negative means you owe it.

If the business owes you, settle with one dated reimbursement from the business to you. If
you owe the business, repay only where the entity treats it as owed (for example a company);
a sole trader's draws are recorded, not repaid. Confirm with the accountant before any
transfer, then record it in the worksheet.
