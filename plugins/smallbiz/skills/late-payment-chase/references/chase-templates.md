Sort twice: by days overdue (oldest first), then by amount (largest first). Work from
the top of whichever view shows the larger risk. Total the overdue amount and compare it
with the cash floor in the cash forecast.

## Message templates by rung

Replace bracketed items. Keep each message to a few lines.

Three days before due:
Subject: Invoice [number] due [date]
Hello [name], a reminder that invoice [number] for [amount] is due on [date]. It is
attached, with the purchase order reference [ref] if one is needed. Please let me know if
anything is missing.

Day 1:
Subject: Invoice [number] - payment date
Hello [name], invoice [number] for [amount] was due yesterday. Could you confirm it has
been approved and tell me the date it will be paid?

Day 7 call, followed by an email:
Subject: Recap of our call
Hello [name], thank you for speaking today. As agreed, [amount] for invoice [number]
will be paid on [date] by [method].

Day 14:
Subject: Overdue invoice [number] - notice
Hello [name], invoice [number] for [amount], due [date], is now [n] days overdue. Under
[clause or statute and source], [fee or interest] applies and is added: total [amount].
Please pay by [date]. As the agreement allows, work is paused from [date] until payment
is received, and delivery dates move accordingly.

Day 30:
Subject: Final demand - invoice [number]
Hello [name], invoice [number] for [amount], due [date], remains unpaid after the
reminders of [dates]. Unless payment of [total] is received by [date, 7 days from today],
I will [refer the matter to collections / start a small-claims or money claim /
propose mediation]. Payment details are attached.
