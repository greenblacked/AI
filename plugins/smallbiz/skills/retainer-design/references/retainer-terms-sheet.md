# Retainer terms sheet

One page, attached to or referenced by the services agreement.

## Terms sheet

- Client / start date / term: [rolling monthly, or fixed months]
- Type: [reserved capacity / hours bank / outcome subscription]
- Monthly fee: [amount], invoiced [in advance on the 1st], due [days]
- Covers: [N hours per month, or list of deliverables]
- Overage: [rate per hour], only with written approval beforehand [yes/no]
- Response time: [initial reply within N business hours by channel]; urgent requests outside these hours are [excluded / charged at rate]
- Excluded: [new projects, third-party costs, out-of-hours work, anything not listed above]
- Rollover: [none] or [unused hours carry over for one month, capped at N]
- Notice: 30 days in writing from either side
- Indexation: fee reviewed on [anniversary], increase of [percentage or rule]
- Review: [date, three months from start], with hours used versus paid
- Discount and what it was traded for: [percentage] for [term or payment schedule], ending if [condition]

## Three-month review checklist

- Hours used divided by hours paid, per month
- Requests declined or delayed because of the cap
- Hours spent on out-of-scope requests
- Effective hourly rate on the retainer, compared with project work
- Decision: continue, resize up, resize down, change type, end
