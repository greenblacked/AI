# Clause guide

This guide describes what each clause is for. Wording and enforceability vary by jurisdiction; use it to ask questions, not to draft final text.

## What a good clause does

| Clause | What to look for | Weak looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Payment terms | Amount or rate, invoice trigger, days to pay, payment method | "Payment on satisfactory completion" |
| Late fee or interest | A stated interest rate or reference to the statutory regime, plus right to recover collection costs where permitted | Silent, or a rate so high it may not be enforceable |
| Deposit | A percentage due before work, stated as non-refundable in whole or part | "Deposit may be refunded at client's discretion" |
| Kill fee | A fee for cancellation, scaled to work done, plus payment for work completed | No cancellation payment, or only payment for delivered milestones |
| IP transfer | Rights pass on receipt of full payment; until then a licence at most | Rights pass on delivery, or on creation |
| Pre-existing tools | You keep your tools, libraries and templates; the client gets a licence to use them within the deliverable | "All materials used in the services are owned by the client" |
| Portfolio rights | You may show the work and name the client unless confidentiality specifically prevents it | Silent, or a blanket ban |
| Confidentiality | Mutual, defined information, stated term, standard exclusions | One-way, perpetual, undefined |
| Liability cap | Your total liability capped at fees paid, excluding indirect loss | Unlimited, or capped at an amount far above the fee |
| Client materials | The client warrants it may supply them and indemnifies you for claims | You warrant that all materials, including theirs, infringe nothing |
| Termination | Written notice by either side, payment for work done to the date | Only the client may terminate, or termination without payment |
| Change procedure | Changes agreed in writing, with price and date effect | Client may change scope on request |
| Disputes and jurisdiction | A stated escalation path, then court or arbitration in a named place | Courts of the client's home when you are abroad, with no escalation |
| Subcontracting | You may subcontract with the same confidentiality and IP terms | Prior written approval for any help |

## Warning signs in client-supplied paper

- Defined terms that widen the indemnity ("claims, losses, costs, whether direct or indirect").
- "Work made for hire" or "all rights, whether now existing or future" with no carve-out for what you brought.
- "Pay when paid" or "subject to receipt of funds from the end customer".
- "Exclusive" or "shall not provide similar services to any other party".
- Payment terms in a schedule or linked document that differ from the main body.
- "Client may amend the specification at any time without adjustment".
- "Revisions until the client is satisfied".
- Auto-renewal with a long notice window.
- A clause that lets the client change the terms by posting to a website.

## Solicitor question template

```text
Clause [n] ([short title]) says: "[exact words]".
My concern: [what could happen to me in practice].
What I want to know: [one specific question: is this enforceable here, what would a
reasonable alternative be, what cap or limit is usual].
Priority: [walk-away | negotiate | accept].
```

Group questions by priority so the solicitor can work from the top. Attach the whole agreement, not only extracts, because clauses interact.
