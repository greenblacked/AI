## Table layout and worked example

Invented figures, one quarter.

| Client | Revenue | Hours | Effective rate | Avg days late | Revisions and changes | Share |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 18,000 | 240 | 75 | 3 | 2 | 60% |
| B | 6,000 | 60 | 100 | 0 | 1 | 20% |
| C | 4,500 | 90 | 50 | 38 | 9 | 15% |
| D | 1,500 | 12 | 125 | 0 | 0 | 5% |

Target rate: 90.

Reading it:

- A is 60% of revenue: schedule action on the concentration, and raise the rate as its effective rate is below target.
- B is on target with low pain: keep.
- C earns 50 an hour, pays 38 days late and has nine changes: restructure with a deposit, shorter terms and a revision cap, with exit as the fallback if there is no change next quarter.
- D is a small, high-rate client: keep, and look for more like it.

The table shows why revenue alone misleads: A and C look important, B and D look minor.

## Pain scale

Score each client 1 to 5 on three things and add them: how often requests arrive outside hours, how often decisions change, how uncomfortable you are showing the work (5 is always the worst end). A total of 12 or more is high pain. Write one sentence of evidence beside each score so the number is defensible later.

## Rate notice draft

```text
Subject: Change to my rates from [date]

Hello [name],

Thank you for the work together over the past [period]. From [date, 60 to 90 days
ahead] my rate for [service] will be [new rate]. Work agreed or started before
[grandfathered date] stays at the current rate until it is delivered.

I review my rates annually and this reflects [one factual reason: scope grown, costs,
experience]. If you would like to talk it through, or reshape the work to fit a budget,
I am happy to.

[name]
```

## Restructure proposal draft

```text
Subject: Proposal for how we work together next quarter

Hello [name],

To keep the work running smoothly I propose three changes from [date]:
1. [A monthly retainer of x hours for y, with unused hours lapsing / rolling over].
2. [A deposit of z% before each new piece of work].
3. [Payment terms of 14 days, with a late fee as set out in our agreement].

I think this gives you predictable cost and quicker turnaround. Could we speak this
week to agree it?
```

## Exit notice draft

Check the agreement's termination clause first and follow its notice period and form.

```text
Subject: Ending our work together

Hello [name],

I am writing to give notice that I will stop taking on work for [company] from
[date, per agreement]. Until then I will complete [committed deliverables] and
prepare a handover covering [files, access, documentation].

I can recommend [peer], who works in the same area and has capacity. I am happy to
introduce you.

Thank you for the opportunity.

[name]
```

Keep the exit brief. A reason is optional; if given, keep it to one sentence about fit or capacity, not the client's behaviour.
