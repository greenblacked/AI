# Templates

Keep each message short. Edit to your voice.

## First reply: the five questions

> Thanks for getting in touch. Before I can suggest an approach I need five things:
>
> 1. Who will sign the agreement, and who pays the invoices?
> 2. What budget range have you set aside?
> 3. What is the deadline, and what is behind it?
> 4. What happens if this is not done?
> 5. Are you speaking to other suppliers?
>
> A few lines is plenty. I will reply within two working days.

## Paid discovery offer

> Rather than quote on assumptions, I suggest a paid discovery: [duration], fixed fee [amount], delivering [written scope and recommendation]. It is yours to keep whether or not we work together. If we proceed, [credit or no credit, decided in advance] applies to the project. I can start on receipt of the deposit.

## Deposit first

> I start every project on receipt of a [percentage] deposit, with the balance invoiced at the milestones in the agreement. Once I have the deposit and the signed scope I can book the start date.

## Decline

> Thank you for thinking of me. I do not think I am the right fit for this one, so I will not be able to take it on. [Optional: X may be a better fit.] I wish you well with it.

## Reply to a spec-work request

> I do not do unpaid samples of the actual deliverable, but I can show you [existing work in the field], walk you through how I would approach it on a call, or run a small paid pilot. Which of these would help you decide?
