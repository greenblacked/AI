## The one-page layout

```text
STATEMENT OF WORK - [project name]
Between [client] and [supplier] · under the services agreement dated [date]
Version [n] · [date]

1. Deliverables
   D1 [name] - accepted when [check a third party could run]
   D2 [name] - accepted when [check]

2. Exclusions (not included unless added by change procedure)
   - [most plausible assumption]
   - [second]
   - [third]

3. Client responsibilities
   - [item] by [date]
   - Feedback within [n] business days of each delivery
   - Decision-maker: [name], whose written approval is final
   - If a client date is missed, supplier dates move by the same number of days.

4. Revisions
   [n] rounds included. One round = one consolidated written list of changes
   from the decision-maker. Further rounds at [rate].

5. Changes
   Any change to items 1 to 3 or to dates is described, estimated and agreed in
   writing before work on it begins. Price and dates adjust in the same document.

6. Acceptance
   A deliverable is accepted if the client does not object in writing within [n]
   business days of delivery, naming the criterion not met.

Signed: [client] [date]   [supplier] [date]   Deposit received: [date]
```

## Sample acceptance criteria by work type

| Work type | Criterion a third party can run |
| --- | --- |
| Website | Every page listed in the sitemap loads on the staging address; the contact form delivers to the stated address within one minute; the site displays without horizontal scrolling at the listed screen widths |
| Design | The logo is delivered in vector and PNG forms in the listed variants (full colour, single colour, reversed), each openable without a font install |
| Writing | The article is the stated length (plus or minus 10%), cites the sources supplied, and has passed one round of changes from the decision-maker |
| Data work | The delivered file reproduces the totals in the supplied control sheet to the unit, and the script runs end to end on the sample input |

## Kickoff recap layout

Subject: Recap of kickoff - [project]

- What success looks like, in your words: "[client's phrase]" and "[client's phrase]"
- What I will deliver, and when: [deliverables with dates]
- What I need from you: [item - date]
- Who decides: [name]
- Next three dates: [list]
- Please reply "confirmed" or send corrections by [date].
