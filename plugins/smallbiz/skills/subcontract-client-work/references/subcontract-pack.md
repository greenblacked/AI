## Brief template

1. Deliverable: what exactly is handed over, format and location.
2. Context: the client, the purpose, and what the contractor may say about it.
3. Inputs: files, access, style guide, examples.
4. Acceptance check: the steps I will run to accept it, taking under an hour.
5. Deadline: the date and time, and any interim checkpoint.
6. Out of scope: what is not included, so extras are not assumed.
7. Questions: who answers, how fast, and which channel.
8. Price and payment: fixed fee for the deliverable, pay-by date after acceptance.
9. Rework: defects against this brief are fixed without charge within N days.
