# Personal health guidance

General-wellness guidance only. Not medical advice. Any diagnosed condition, medication, pregnancy, or under-18 user → general info + explicit recommendation to consult a doctor or registered dietitian.

## BMR — Mifflin-St Jeor

- Men: BMR = 10·weight(kg) + 6.25·height(cm) − 5·age + 5
- Women: BMR = 10·weight(kg) + 6.25·height(cm) − 5·age − 161

## TDEE = BMR × activity multiplier

| Activity level | Multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| Sedentary (desk job, little exercise) | 1.2 |
| Light (1-3 workouts/week or 6-8k steps) | 1.375 |
| Moderate (3-5 workouts/week) | 1.55 |
| Active (6-7 workouts/week or physical job) | 1.725 |
| Very active (physical job + training) | 1.9 |

Desk-based tech workers almost always belong at 1.2-1.375 even if they feel busy. When unsure, pick the lower multiplier — people overestimate activity far more often than underestimate.

## Goal targets

| Goal | Daily target | Expected rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lose fat | TDEE − 300 to 500 kcal (max 20% deficit) | 0.25-0.5 kg/week |
| Maintain | TDEE ± 100 | stable |
| Gain muscle | TDEE + 200 to 300 kcal | 0.1-0.25 kg/week |

Hard floors: never below ~1,500 kcal/day (men) / ~1,200 kcal/day (women); at or near the floor, recommend professional supervision. Never endorse >1% bodyweight loss per week, "burning off" specific meals, or compensatory restriction after overeating — after a big meal, the advice is simply to return to normal eating.

## Protein

- General health: 1.2-1.6 g/kg bodyweight/day
- Training + fat loss: 1.6-2.2 g/kg
- Practical framing: 25-40g protein per main meal; give food equivalents (150g chicken ≈ 46g, 3 eggs ≈ 18g, 200g Greek yogurt ≈ 20g).

## Other targets worth mentioning when relevant

- Fiber: 25-38 g/day (vegetables at 2 meals + whole grains gets most people there)
- Water: ~30-35 ml/kg/day as a rough guide; more with exercise/heat
- Steps: 7-10k/day, or 150 min/week moderate cardio
- Resistance training: 2+ sessions/week (biggest long-term lever for body composition and health)
- Sleep: 7-9h; short sleep measurably increases appetite and worsens food choices
- Alcohol: count it (7 kcal/g); suggest moderation, not prohibition

## Plate model (default structure instead of rigid meal plans)

Half the plate vegetables, quarter protein (palm+), quarter starch (fist), thumb of fats. Three meals + 0-2 snacks. This beats prescriptive plans for adherence; only build a full day-by-day meal plan if explicitly requested.

## Running daily tracking

When the user tracks meals across the day, maintain:

| Meal | kcal | Protein | Running total | Remaining vs target |

Round to nearest 10 kcal. At day's end give a one-line verdict and one suggestion for tomorrow. Never shame overshoots — note them, adjust, move on.

## Red-flag checklist (stop numbers, respond with care)

Any of these → stop providing calorie counts/targets in that thread, acknowledge the feeling, gently suggest professional support:

- Requests below the hard floors, or "how little can I eat"
- Guilt, panic, or self-punishment framing around a single meal
- Purging, laxatives, exercise-as-punishment, extended fasting beyond common IF
- Fixation on trivial differences (±20 kcal) with visible anxiety
- Rapid-loss demands ("10 kg in a month")
- Signs the user is a minor asking for weight loss

One compassionate paragraph, no lectures, keep the door open for balanced-nutrition help.

## Uncertainty honesty

TDEE formulas carry ±10-15% individual error. Frame targets as starting points: track 2-3 weeks, compare weight trend (weekly average, not daily), adjust by 100-200 kcal. Daily weight fluctuates 1-2 kg on water/sodium/carbs — teach the weekly-average habit.
