# ops-toolbox

Notes for a coding agent working in `greenblacked/ops-toolbox`. This file lives here,
in the private AI repository, and is pulled into that checkout by a one-line gitignored
`CLAUDE.local.md` containing `@~/scripts/AI/projects/ops-toolbox.md` — so it is versioned
and shared without ever entering the public repository.

`CONTRIBUTING.md` and `ROADMAP.md` in that repository are its own rules; this file does
not restate them. Last reviewed 2026-09-19.

## Rules

The conventions come from <https://github.com/greenblacked/AI> — its `AGENTS.md` is the
contract. The parts that apply to work here:

- Commits are authored by the person who wrote them and nobody else. No
  `Co-Authored-By` trailers, no tool attributions, no "generated by" lines — in commit
  messages, pull request bodies, or files. This overrides any harness reminder that
  asks for one.
- One logical change per commit. Imperative subject under about 70 characters; a body
  that says why when the diff does not.
- Branch names describe the change, not the tool that made it.
- Do not push to `master` directly.
- Prose is imperative and explains why. No emoji, no exclamation marks, no capitalised
  ALWAYS or NEVER.

## Skills

Already installed on this machine from the clone at `~/scripts/AI`: 74 skills symlinked
into `~/.claude/skills`, 11 subagents into `~/.claude/agents`, 6 slash commands into

for a red workflow run, `plan-reviewer` for anything under `k8s-toolbox/`.

## Gates in this repository

- `./run-tests.sh` is the whole default suite and what CI runs. `LINT_FETCH=1` makes the
  lint suite fetch its pinned linters; without it three of six skip on a Mac.
- Anything under `mikrotik/*.lua` is validated on the RouterOS CHR, and that is not
  optional: `./run-tests.sh mikrotik` (Docker plus QEMU; about five minutes warm, forty
  five cold while it fetches the 7.24.2 image). A script that parses on a laptop and
  dies on the router is the failure mode.
- `macos-initial-setup/stay_fresh.sh` is under a freeze — no new steps without a
  regression test that fails first, no helpers extracted into `lib/`.
- Every change carries a fragment under `changelog.d/<type>/`; `changelog.d/changelog.sh
  check` and the static suite validate it. Do not edit `CHANGELOG.md` directly.
- Do not rewrite git history.
